TOH3 matches W4, DMOSpeech 2's code default, on clean quality and pitch variety for one pass fewer (12 against 13).

**Noisy prompts:**
- **gauss25 (white noise at 25 dB):** UTMOS is 3.599 for TOH3, against 3.647 for the student alone and 2.438 for W4.
- **noisy15 (15 dB):** 1.858, 1.882 and 1.249. At 15 dB the student alone is also poor, so blocking the teacher's noise only brings TOH3 to the student's level.
- **Runaway outputs (WER > 1) at noisy15:** none for TOH3, TOH2 or the student; 1 for W4, 3 for W3 and 4 for W2.

**Telephone prompts (exploratory):** every arm is between 1.57 and 1.71 UTMOS, and TOH3 (1.616) equals the student alone (1.613) and W4 (1.617). The student alone does not recover these prompts either, so there was nothing for TOH to block here.

## Post hoc diagnostics (descriptive, outside the verdict)

Computed by `code/analysis/exp030_diagnostics.py` into `results/diagnostics.json` (sha256 `624a95f32b70...`).

**The mirror control does about as well as TOH3:**
- **Pitch variety:** MIR3 − TOH3 is −0.0030 [−0.0088, +0.0023], with 10 of 20 speakers above zero. MIR3 − STU is +0.0240 [+0.0187, +0.0302].
- **UTMOS, TOH3 − MIR3:**
  - clean −0.012 [−0.075, +0.054];
  - pooled over the two noise conditions +0.023 [−0.029, +0.074];
  - noisy15 +0.072 [−0.009, +0.161];
  - telephone +0.044 [−0.018, +0.109].
- **Similarity:** the paired mean difference is about zero. Under Definition 3.1 against W8, MIR3 had more similarity failures than TOH3: 5 against 1 on clean prompts and 8 against 3 on telephone prompts. That is a hint, not a test.

**What the mirror result means:** a re-timing of the same size, put where the teacher did not put it, gives the same variety gain. So this pilot shows that *re-timing the student's draft per seed* restores the teacher's level of CVf0. It does not show that the teacher's *particular* timing is what matters.

**Why CVf0 cannot separate the two:** CVf0 compares seeds frame by frame without aligning them (`run_selective_handover_prelim.cvf0`). Seeds whose timing differs therefore raise CVf0 even when their pitch contours agree. The same applies to the W arms' CVf0. A listening test or an alignment-aware variety measure would be needed to say how much of the "pitch variety" is timing.

**Displacement and variety:** across the 20 diversity speakers, the CVf0 gain over the student does not track the mean map displacement. The Spearman correlations are TOH3 +0.12, MIR3 −0.01, W3 +0.22, TOH2 +0.00 and W2 +0.27. The mean displacement of one map is not the same thing as how much the maps differ across seeds, so this is weak evidence either way.

**The time maps:**
- **Size:** TOH3's mean RMS displacement is 11.6 frames on clean prompts and 12.4–13.6 on degraded ones, about 3% of the length; the band is about 45 frames.
- **Band edge:** the largest displacement reaches the band in 1–4 of 30 cases per condition.

**Definition 3.1 failures against W8:** at noisy15, W4 has 17, TOH3 8 and the student alone 5; at gauss25, W4 2, TOH3 1 and the student alone 2. Definition 3.1 measures against W8, which copies the prompt's noise, so it counts some of the student's cleaner outputs as failures. That is a protocol question for the team.

## What this means

- **Under the frozen rule, TOH3 is supported as a development result.** At 12 passes it gives W4's clean quality and variety, and on white-noise prompts it keeps the student alone's quality instead of the teacher's copied noise, with no runaway outputs.
- **Not shown:** that the teacher's timing, rather than any per-seed re-timing of that size, carries the variety. This matters for the next step: if a re-timing made without the teacher works as well, the 8 teacher passes add nothing that was measured.
- **Not shown:** an advantage over SNR routing on conditions that SNR cannot detect. On telephone prompts no arm differed.
- **Development evidence only.** No held-out speakers, no listening test, and no adviser review.
