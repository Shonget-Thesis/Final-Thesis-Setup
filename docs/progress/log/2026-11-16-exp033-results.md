## What this means

**The answer to the request** (keep the 0.25 variety and quality, with fewer teacher passes):
- **On clean prompts, `CPH8` does it at the point-estimate level:** 12 passes instead of the paper's 30 (60% fewer passes, and 60% less time on the T4).
- **With the SNR gate** sending noisy prompts to the student alone, the mean over these 60 cases is 8 passes, against 30 for the paper's setting and 19.2 for the thesis's current policy. Noisy-prompt quality is then better than both.

**What it is, and is not:**
- **Not a new mechanism:** `CPH8` is the released sampler with `teacher_steps = 8`, the paper's 0.25 stop and the paper's two-step student restart. No operator is added.
- **What is new** is the finding behind it: the handover should happen where the teacher has decided the melody (t ≈ 0.29) with the student restarted there, and the path to that point can be walked in half the steps.
- **The routing direction** (noisy prompts to the student alone) comes from EXP-029 to EXP-032.

**What is not yet shown:**
- **Confirmation:** nothing is confirmed at the 90% bar. This is one development run in which ten arms were judged and the cheapest match was picked, so `CPH8` needs a preregistered test on new speakers.
- **UTMOS against the thesis's `W8`:** every arm that matches the paper has the paper's UTMOS (about 3.7), which is about 0.2 below `W8` and the student. A listening test is still missing.
- **Noisy prompts between 18 and 22 dB,** where the gate is known to miss (EXP-011), were not in this run.
