## Findings (development evidence)

- **The problem holds on both grids.**
  - Early fixed switching fails mainly on noisy prompts: 16-interval Step 4 fails 2 of 60 clean inputs against 126 of 300 noisy ones; 32-interval s = 0.07 fails 2 of 60 against 129 of 300.
  - The cheapest safe step varies per input. Its hindsight saving is 29.7% (16-interval) and 33.7% (32-interval).
- **The failure rule has a noise floor on noisy prompts.** The two references have equal mean quality, yet they flag each other on 81 and 78 of 300 noisy inputs, against 0 and 2 of 60 clean ones.
- **Definition 3.4 (the full policy, 20 dB gate) adds little.**
  - On the 16-interval grid it certifies no threshold in any fold, and in none of 100 re-splits.
  - On the 32-interval grid it saves 1.8%, with no difference from a cost-matched random rule (McNemar p = 0.77).
  - Calibrated on clean prompts only, it fails on 28.0% (16-interval) and 5.0% (32-interval) of noisy prompts.
- **Within the 5% limit:**
  - the calibrated SNR gate saves 10.9% (16-interval) and 13.4% (32-interval);
  - SNR + TAS saves 14.0% on both grids;
  - the full policy with the Objective 1 gate saves 7.0% and 9.1%.
- **The paper's settings are worse than the thesis reference.** `paper` adds 140 failures and `alg2` 172 (noisy WER 0.34 and 0.41, against 0.076). `alg2`'s speaker similarity is lower than `paper`'s (−0.013, p = 3.5 × 10⁻⁸). The 32-interval reference `s025` matches the thesis reference in quality at 57% more passes.
- **Teacher-only and student-only.** Teacher-only is the worst (noisy WER 0.97). Student-only (4 passes) matches the reference on clean prompts and beats it on noisy ones (SIM +0.077, UTMOS 2.69 against 1.46). It still adds 59 failures under Definition 3.1, and diversity is not measured.

## Consequences (not applied; team and adviser decisions)

- **The 32-interval question:** the paper's 14 + 2 settings, literal or with the released handover, give a worse reference than the thesis's 16-interval Step 8. The paper's teacher schedule with the thesis handover gives the same quality at a higher cost.
- **The drift signal:** Definition 3.4 is a tested negative result on this development set. The thesis plan already treats the drift signal as a hypothesis; the SNR-gated variant and SNR + TAS are the methods that saved compute within the limit.
- **The failure rule's noise floor** on noisy prompts should be stated wherever noisy-prompt failure counts are reported, including Appendix D.
- **Student-only's quality** on these metrics needs an answer in the proposal, for example DMOSpeech 2's diversity argument.
