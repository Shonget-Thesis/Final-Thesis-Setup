## Findings (development evidence)

- SNR + TAS: 12/360 failures (3.3%) at 18.20 NFE against SNR-only 86/360 at 14.40 (74 fixed, 0 new, p ≈ 1e-22); a tie with the simple drift veto and a non-significant trade-off with the calibrated SNR gate (7/360 at 18.71; paired p 0.23).
- SNR-Wiener Conditioning: noisy-prompt speech at Step 4 beats the full teacher (similarity +0.126, better in 90% of 300 cases; WER −0.048); noisy Step-4 failures 126 → 21; SNR + SWC 20/360 at 13.00 NFE (fewer failures and less compute than SNR-only; risk 5.6%) or 11/360 at 16.98 NFE routed (within the limit). It matches a pretrained enhancer without an extra network.
- Did not win: SNNG, SCG, PIEC (output-side cancellation), the antithetic pair.
- Preregistered criteria: C1 passed for SWC and the enhancer variants; C2 and C3 failed (C3 compares with the calibrated SNR gate; C2 is inflated by the hardware difference).
- Hardware: the A100 reproduced the teacher exactly but not the stored T4 student outputs (GPU-dependent student noise). TAS, SNR-only, the calibrated gate and the drift veto are unaffected; new-method failures are inflated.
