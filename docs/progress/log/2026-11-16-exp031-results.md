**1. The teacher is not shown to be needed.** The random re-timing (`RND`, 4 passes, no teacher) matches every candidate on raw CVf0 and on clean UTMOS. The README set out this outcome in advance: random re-timing does as well, so these measures do not justify the teacher's passes, and a teacher-free route is a team decision.

**2. "Beats all previous" does not credit the teacher.** This was checked after the results were seen, with the frozen M function (`code/analysis/exp031_diagnostics.py`):
- **The student alone (4 passes)** also beats all five previous methods.

**6. The Blind-Teacher Handover (BTH3).**
- **Correction to the design claim.** The design log and README said no property of the prompt can reach the blind teacher. That is not so:
  - **Through the target length:** the blind teacher receives the target length, which the pipeline's duration estimate sets from the prompt. Against the clean prompt, the length differed for 19 to 25 of the 30 speakers in each degraded condition, by −200 to +120 frames, while the prompt's own length never changed.
  - **Through the student's draft:** BTH's map is aligned to S₀, which hears the prompt.
  - **What does hold:** only the teacher's estimate at a given length is free of the prompt.
  - **Where it is corrected:** the README and the design log. The runner's docstring makes the same overstatement and is left unchanged, because its hash is part of the frozen preregistration.
- **Compute:** the blind teacher ran on 0.504 of the full sequence on average (0.31–0.66). Its 8 teacher passes plus 4 student passes are then about 8.0 full-length pass equivalents if cost is linear in length, or 6.1 if quadratic. This is arithmetic on recorded lengths; no per-arm time was measured.
- **Conditions:**
  - **C not shown:** −0.008.
  - **reverb03:** −0.069 [−0.178, +0.032] against W4, where TOH3 had +0.050.
  - **babble15:** 10 Definition 3.1 failures against W8, all on similarity (TOH3: 1). BTH3 − TOH3 similarity on babble15 is −0.0077 [−0.0164, +0.0017]. Unexplained.
- **Map size:** BTH3's maps move frames about twice as far as TOH3's (21.1 against 11.0 frames RMS). Across the 20 speakers, its aligned-CVf0 gain does not track map size:
  - Spearman −0.04;
  - BTH3 − TOH3 against the difference in map size: −0.18.
  - So there is no sign that the gain is a warping artefact, but with 20 speakers this is weak evidence.

## What this means

- **Under the frozen rule, no candidate shows that its teacher is needed.** TOH3 and BTH3 beat all five previous methods, and TOH3 shows condition safety. But the student alone passes the same M test at 4 passes, and a teacher-free random re-timing gives the same raw pitch variety and clean UTMOS. On these measures the 8 teacher passes are not justified.
- **The compute question now turns on whether pitch variety matters.**
  - **Quality:** on UTMOS, WER and similarity, the student alone (4 passes) is no worse than any teacher-based method on clean prompts and much better on white noise.
  - **Variety:** the teacher's measurable contribution is aligned pitch variety (W4: +0.0175 over the student). The arms that carry more of it have lower UTMOS.
  - **Open:** whether that variety is wanted, and whether UTMOS penalises it unfairly, needs a listening test. It is a team decision.
- **What remains of the candidates (exploratory):**
  - **QH3 and BTH3:** carry part of the teacher's non-timing pitch variety (64% and 46% of W4's) without its copied white noise.
  - **QH3:** does so at W4's clean UTMOS.
  - **BTH3:** runs its teacher on about half the sequence.
  - **To confirm:** aligned CVf0 as the preregistered primary measure, on speakers not used here, plus a listening test.
- **A limit of the frozen T1 test.** T1 used raw CVf0, which counts timing differences as variety. RND's maps differ more across seeds (timing spread 12.6 frames, against 10.3–10.7 for the candidates), which gave it as much raw CVf0. In hindsight T1 could not separate the candidates from random timing. The aligned measure was exploratory and cannot be promoted after seeing the data.
- **Development evidence only.** No held-out speakers, no listening test, and no adviser review. Chapter 3 is unchanged.

## Remaining blockers and decisions

  - speakers not used here;
  - a listening test;
