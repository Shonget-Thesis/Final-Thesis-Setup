**1. The paper's setting has much more pitch variety than the thesis's `W8`.**
- **The size:** aligned CVf0 is 0.0881 for `P25` and 0.0576 for `W8`, so `W8` keeps about a third of the paper setting's gain over the student alone.
- **Per speaker:** `P25` is above `W8` on aligned CVf0 for 19 of the 20 speakers (post hoc).
- **So:** the thesis's reference matches the paper's switching time, not the paper's variety.

**2. On the released grid, variety stops growing after Step 2 or 3.** Post hoc speaker-level differences:

| Difference | CVf0 | Aligned CVf0 | Timing spread |
|---|---|---|---|
| `W2` − `W1` | +0.0145 [−0.0060, +0.0438] | +0.0033 [−0.0140, +0.0307] | +2.30 [+1.36, +3.38] |
| `W3` − `W2` | +0.0046 [+0.0005, +0.0098] | −0.0003 [−0.0057, +0.0053] | +1.28 [+0.71, +1.96] |
| `W4` − `W3` | +0.0010 [−0.0042, +0.0059] | +0.0013 [−0.0027, +0.0061] | +0.18 [−0.19, +0.54] |
| `W8` − `W4` | −0.0006 [−0.0030, +0.0019] | −0.0009 [−0.0043, +0.0019] | −0.08 [−0.28, +0.12] |

- **The plateau:** from Step 3 on, more teacher passes add no measured variety of any kind.
- **Aligned pitch variety** stops growing even earlier, after Step 1.
- **Relevance to Chapter 3:** its candidates (Steps 4, 5, 6) lose no measured variety against its reference, Step 8.

**3. How the student restarts matters more than how the teacher hands over (exploratory).**
- **The handover mechanism doesn't matter:** `P25` − `P25R` is within ±0.003 on both pitch measures, with intervals that include zero. Continuing from the teacher's state or re-noising its estimate gives the same variety.
- **The restart level does.** Post hoc, `P25R` − `W8`:
  - CVf0: +0.0238 [+0.0081, +0.0476], higher for 19 of 20 speakers;
  - aligned CVf0: +0.0313 [+0.0135, +0.0556], higher for 20 of 20 speakers.
- **How the two arms differ:**
  - **The restart level:** both re-noise a teacher estimate, but `P25R`'s student starts at 0.2929, while `W8`'s starts at 0.0761 (the thesis's fixed j = 1). At 0.0761, 92% of the student's input is fresh noise.
  - **The teacher's progress:** `W8`'s teacher went further (t = 0.2929, against `P25R`'s 0.2270), so the missing variety is not missing teacher work.
  - **A confound:** the arms also use different grids, so this contrast alone does not isolate the restart level.
- **What crosses at the low restart:** from Step 3 on, the `W` arms keep 85–88% of `P25`'s gain in timing spread, but only about 35% of its aligned pitch variety. Read together: at the low restart the teacher's timing reaches the output, and its pitch contour mostly does not.

**4. Pitch variety comes with lower predicted naturalness.**
- **`P25` against `W8`:** `P25`'s UTMOS is 0.176 [0.046, 0.348] below `W8`'s, and its similarity is lower too (0.9348 against 0.9449).
- **`P25R` against `W8`:** −0.159 [−0.321, −0.028] UTMOS.
- **The teacher alone** (`TCH`) has the lowest UTMOS of all (3.155) and the lowest WER (0.0171).
- **The pattern** matches EXP-029 and EXP-031: arms with more teacher variety have lower UTMOS.
- **Caution:** UTMOS is a predictor. Whether it penalises expressive prosody or real artefacts is not known without a listening test.

**5. Pitch variety on noisy15 was not measured.**
- **What happened:** on noisy15, pYIN voices 0% of frames (in at least half the seeds) for every teacher arm, because those outputs copy the white noise.
- **Defined values:**
  - CVf0 is defined for none of the 20 speakers in `W1`, `W2`, `W3` and `P25`, and for at most one in `W4`, `W8` and `P25R`;
  - the student alone voices 18% and is defined for 14.
- **So:** the means are undefined, which is why `noisy_cheapest_step_point` is `none`. The README listed this as a known limit.
- **Timing spread** stays defined: every arm's rises on noisy15. On noisy prompts it cannot separate speech timing from copied noise.

**6. On noisy15 no teacher arm keeps the student's quality.**
- **UTMOS:** every teacher arm is 0.65 to 0.72 below the student alone (1.25–1.32 against 1.97), as in EXP-031.
- **WER:** among the teacher arms `W8` has the lowest (0.175 mean, one runaway), against 0.561 for `P25` and 3.27 for `W1` (eight runaways).

## What this means

**For Chapter 3's reference.**
- **What Chapter 3 says:** the reference is s = 0.25, "matching the paper's switching time" ("Candidate Switching Times"), and the student restart is fixed at j = 1 for every switching time ("Baseline Sampler").
- **What EXP-032 adds:** with j = 1, Step 8 keeps about a third of the paper setting's aligned pitch variety gain over the student alone.
- **Nothing is changed in the manuscript.** Whether to say this in Chapter 3, or to revisit j, is a team decision.
- **EVAL-009:** the j = 2 comparison it asks for was largely run in EXP-026–029, at Steps 4, 5, 6 and 8 (point 8). It was not run as EVAL-009 specifies (all 100 development speakers in one run, with each setting's own reference). With EXP-032, `W8R2` is the 16-interval setting that matches the paper's 0.25 in quality and, approximately, in variety.

**For SNR + drift.**
- **Against the thesis's reference:** in the thesis's pipeline, stopping at Step 4, 5 or 6 instead of Step 8 loses no measured variety (`W4` ≈ `W8`).
- **Against the paper:** every `W` arm sits at the same plateau, so all are below the paper's setting.

**For the next handover design.**
- **The lever:** under j = 1, the variety that the paper's setting adds is decided by the student's restart, not by the number of teacher steps. EXP-026's transmissibility result had already shown this: the restart level sets a ceiling that more teacher steps cannot raise.
- **Under j = 2 the curve is known** (EXP-026–029):
  - **Variety:** it grows with teacher steps, from `W4R2` to `W8R2`.
  - **UTMOS:** it moves the other way, a monotone trade-off.
  - **The paper's variety** costs about 20 passes on the 16-interval grid (`W8R2`).
- **So a new handover would have to beat the `W_kR2` curve, not `W8`:** the paper's variety for fewer than 20 passes, or at higher quality.
- **The noisy question stays open:** noisy prompts need a variety measure that works on noise-copying outputs before any design can be judged there.
- **An idea already set aside:** splitting the restart by frequency is the FreeInit family. A FreeInit-style noise reshaping was listed on 2026-11-14 among the candidates that mapped onto known techniques.
