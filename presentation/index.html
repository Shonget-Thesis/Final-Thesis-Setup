Background of the study

Speech,
generated from noise

A flow-matching model learns a velocity field that moves random noise toward a mel-spectrogram along a nearly straight path.

dx/dt = vθ(x, t),   t ∈ [0, 1]generation = integrate the field step by step

Every step is one full network evaluation. F5-TTS, our teacher, spaces its steps with sway sampling.

noise · t = 0 mel-spectrogram · t = 1 t = 0.000

Problem statement

One handover for every input

Input Aclean, easy
Input Bmoderate
Input Cnoisy prompt

FIXED HANDOVER th = 0.293 · 21 PASSES EACH

Too late wastes guided passes. Too early may degrade speech. Does every utterance need 18 teacher passes?

1.2.1

Uncharacterized effects. How the switching time changes WER, SIM, UTMOS and cost per utterance is unmeasured.

1.2.2

The handover boundary. Can inference-time signals pick an earlier switch without losing quality?

1.2.3

No reliability guarantee. Adaptive selection alone says nothing about the rate of added failures.

1.2.4

Application constraints. Faster synthesis is not evidence of benefit to autistic users.

Objectives of the study

Choose the switch per utterance,
with a bound on failures

1

Characterize

Measure how WER, SIM, UTMOS, forward passes and runtime vary across candidate switching times and inputs.

→ quality–cost map
2

Formulate

Pose selection as constrained cost minimization; design a training-free policy with no extra network evaluations; prove its properties.

→ policy + 4 propositions
3

Calibrate

Use Learn-Then-Test to keep added-failure risk within a tolerance α at confidence 1 − δ; check it on held-out speakers.

→ certified threshold λ̂
4

Compare

Benchmark SNR-only, stabilization-only and combined variants against fixed switches and an oracle, clean and noisy.

→ does each signal add value?

Significance · Scope and limitations

One variable. Everything else frozen.

INOnly the switching time varies. Student restart level stays at the released default.
INTeacher, student, duration predictor and vocoder stay frozen.
INEnglish zero-shot prompts from Seed-TTS test-en, clean and 15 dB noisy.
INAll runs on an NVIDIA Tesla T4 through Google Colab.
OUTA per-utterance guarantee; the bound holds on average over inputs.
OUTBuilding or testing an assistive app; clinical or learner outcomes.

Statistical risk control and calibration

A promise you can check

P( R(λ̂) ≤ α ) ≥ 1 − δ

“At most 5% added failures, and this promise is wrong with probability at most 10%.”

Learn-Then-Test turns each candidate threshold into a hypothesis test and needs no monotonicity. Testing in a fixed order keeps the overall error at δ.

INPUTS NEEDED · α = 0.05, δ = 0.10, ZERO FAILURES

(1 − α)n ≤ δ  ⇒  n ≥ ln δ / ln(1 − α)
Exact binomial
0
Hoeffding bound
≈0

The guarantee is marginal (on average over inputs) and needs calibration and test inputs from the same distribution.

Formulation and properties

The stopping rule

minπ E[ C(π(x)) ]   s.t.   R(π) = E[ f(x, π(x)) ] ≤ αcheapest policy whose added-failure risk stays under α
πλ(x) = first candidate ir with dir ≤ λ and Ŝ ≥ θ, else referencewalks the teacher steps in order · never later than step 8
Added failure · fixed before calibration

f = 1 if WER > max(12.5%, WERref + 1 pt)  or  SIM < SIMref − 0.02, on the same seeds.

Setting λ = ∞ gives the SNR-only rule; θ = −∞ gives stabilization only. One family, one calibration.

Prop. 3.2

Zero overhead

Signals reuse the velocity already computed. No extra forward pass.

Prop. 3.3

Monotone cost

A smaller λ never stops earlier, so cost rises as λ falls.

Prop. 3.4

Safe fallback

Never more than 21 passes. No certified λ → exact reference output.

Prop. 3.5

Oracle bound

No zero-risk policy beats the cheapest non-failing candidate per input.

The contribution

Per-utterance handover, with a certified bound on added failures

Decide

Stop the teacher once its guess settles and the prompt is clean. Zero extra passes.

Guarantee

λ̂ calibrated so added failures ≤ α with probability ≥ 1 − δ.

Fall back

Never costs more than the released sampler; no certificate means the reference.
