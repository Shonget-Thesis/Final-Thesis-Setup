Speech researchers

A formulation, proofs and calibration for teacher-guided samplers in general.

TTS developers

Lower cost with a stated bound, no retraining.

Assistive-tech developers

Cheaper, predictable synthesis for personalized voices.

Future researchers

A reusable protocol: disjoint calibration, fixed failure rule, exact cost.
