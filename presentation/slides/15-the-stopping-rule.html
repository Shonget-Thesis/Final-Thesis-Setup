Formulation of the Constrained Stopping Policy

minπ E[ C(π(x)) ]   s.t.   R(π) = E[ f(x, π(x)) ] ≤ αcheapest policy whose added-failure risk stays under α
πλ(x) = first candidate ir with dir ≤ λ and Ŝ ≥ θ, else referencewalks the teacher steps in order · never later than step 8
Added failure · fixed before calibration

f = 1 if WER > max(12.5%, WERref + 1 pt)  or  SIM < SIMref − 0.02, on the same seeds.

Setting λ = ∞ gives the SNR-only rule; θ = −∞ gives stabilization only. One family, one calibration.

Prop. 3.2

Zero overhead

Signals reuse the velocity already computed. No extra forward pass.

Prop. 3.3

Monotone cost

A smaller λ never stops earlier, so cost rises as λ falls.

Prop. 3.4

Safe fallback

Never more than 21 passes. No certified λ → exact reference output.

Prop. 3.5

Oracle bound

No zero-risk policy beats the cheapest non-failing candidate per input.
