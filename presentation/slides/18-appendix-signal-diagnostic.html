The SNR separated the two conditions completely; a 20 dB gate sent every noisy prompt to the reference. Fixed early handovers failed on 49–63% of noisy prompts but 2–4% of clean ones.

Stabilization score d₄ on clean prompts

AUC ≈ 0.47 for Step-4 failures vs passes, from only two to three failures. At λ = 0.50 the policy waited past Step 4 on 78 of 100 clean prompts, of which 75–77 would already have passed.
