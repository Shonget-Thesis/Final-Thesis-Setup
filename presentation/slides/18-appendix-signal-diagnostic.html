EXP-012 Discovery · Noise causes premature stabilization

On clean prompts, single-step d4 had AUC ≈ 0.47 (2–3 failures). Across noise levels (n = 360), degraded prompts stabilize prematurely: median d4 drops from 0.61 (clean) to 0.17 (15 dB noisy) as teacher guidance contrast collapses (ρ = 0.89). Thus di ≤ λ stops noisy prompts first.

EXP-013–018 Resolution · Cumulative trajectory action

Eight state-altering operators (DEH, TCR, cDEH, SEA, SNNG, SCG, PIEC) failed routing criterion C3, and 1-cut SNR gates collapsed to 1.5% saving when Step 1 was included. Summing path length Ai = ∑j=1i dj ≥ λ̂i flips the direction, smooths step jitter, and removes Prompt SNR.
