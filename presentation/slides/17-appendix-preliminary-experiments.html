D.2 · Student restart level & paper 14 + 2 baselines

In EXP-017 on the Tesla T4 (n = 360), the paper’s 14 + 2 (j = 2, 32 passes) added 140/360 failures (noisy WER 0.342 vs 0.076) and Algorithm 2 (no re-noising) added 172/360; 32-grid s = 0.25 (33 passes) matched 16-grid Step 8 at +57% cost. Keeping j = 1 (τ = 0.0761, 3 passes) is empirically justified.
