D.2 · Student restart level

Restarting at j = 2 departs from the j = 1 reference more often (26 vs 11 of 48 at s = 0.07; 14 vs 0 at s = 0.25, zero by construction). Small CPU run, not a test of absolute quality. j = 1 is kept as a controlled setting.

D.3 · Stabilization policy, cross-validated

100 development speakers, four folds: 18.88 passes (−10.1%) with 1 added failure in 200, against 50 for a fixed s = 0.15. Chosen with zero training failures, so no risk certificate; Learn-Then-Test replaces this selection.
