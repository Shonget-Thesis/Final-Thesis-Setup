Background of the study

Flow-Matching Formulation for Text-to-Speech

A flow-matching model learns a velocity field that moves random noise toward a mel-spectrogram along a nearly straight path.

dx/dt = vθ(x, t),   t ∈ [0, 1]generation = integrate the field step by step

Every step is one full network evaluation. F5-TTS, our teacher, spaces its steps with sway sampling.
